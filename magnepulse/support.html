MagnePulse Support

Need help with MagnePulse? Write to fulgorstudio7@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can.

How do I play?

Drag a magnet from the bottom onto an empty cell (or tap a magnet, then a cell). A red magnet pushes the nearest block in each of the four directions one cell away; a blue magnet pulls it one cell closer. Blocks lined up together move together. Fill every glowing green socket with a block. Tap anything on the board, or the (?) button, to see what it does.

What is Field Vision?

While you drag a magnet over the board, arrows show where each block would slide. It is free for the first ten levels and part of Master Pro after that.

I’m stuck

Use a hint (the light bulb): it takes back any wrong magnets and shows the next right cell in gold. You get a free hint every day.

Undo

Free players can undo three times per level, and Start again is always free. Master Pro makes undo unlimited.

Restoring a purchase

Open the store (the gold badge button) and tap “Restore purchases”, or use Settings.

I reinstalled the game

If you are signed in to iCloud, your level, stars and settings come back on their own within a few seconds.

No sound or vibration

Open Settings (the sliders button) to check Sound, Volume and Haptics, and your iPhone’s volume and Sounds & Haptics settings.
