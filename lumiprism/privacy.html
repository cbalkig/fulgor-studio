Privacy Policy

LumiPrism Privacy

Last updated: November 3, 2026 · Published by Fulgor Studio

  • No account
  • Stays on your device
  • No tracking
  • No ads
  • No notifications

Summary at a glance

LumiPrism is a puzzle game. It does not need any personal information, we have no accounts and no servers of our own, and nothing you do in the game is sent to us.

What the app accesses

  • Vibration and sound: the game uses your iPhone’s haptic engine and speaker for its sounds. This involves no personal data.
  • LumiPrism does not access your photos, contacts, location, camera, microphone or health data.
  • Levels are made on your device from the level number, so the game needs no server to play.
  • Game Center: if you are signed in to Apple’s Game Center, LumiPrism posts your highest level and total stars to its leaderboards, where you and your Game Center friends can see them. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy; we receive no personal information from it.

What stays on your device

Your level, stars, energy cores, today’s Daily Prism progress, chosen laser style and sound, volume and haptics settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the game removes them from the device. If you are signed in to iCloud, a copy is also kept in your own iCloud (Apple’s key-value storage) so reinstalling the app or moving to a new iPhone picks up where you left off; it is stored by Apple in your account and we cannot see it.

Purchases

The Cyber Pack and energy cores are bought through Apple’s App Store. The game talks to Apple directly to show prices, take payments and restore purchases; we never see your name, email or payment details, and no third party is involved.

LumiPrism does not connect to any server of ours. Apart from the App Store, it works entirely offline.

Notifications

LumiPrism does not send notifications.

Children

LumiPrism is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If anything changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will be described here before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email fulgorstudio7@gmail.com. We read every message.

LumiPrism follows the Fulgor Studio privacy principles shared by all our apps. Need help? Visit LumiPrism Support.
