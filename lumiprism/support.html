How do I play?

Tap a mirror, splitter or prism to turn it a quarter. Guide the lasers so every crystal gets exactly the colour it shows. Light mixes like light: red and green make yellow, red and blue make magenta, green and blue make cyan, and all three make white. A prism joins the beams that enter it and sends the mix out of its point; a splitter lets light through and reflects a copy; a coloured ring only lets its own colour pass.

I’m stuck

Use an angle lock (the gold padlock): it turns one piece to its right angle and locks it there. You get a free lock every day.

Restoring a purchase

Open the store (the lightning button) and tap “Restore purchases”, or use Settings.

No sound or vibration

Open Settings (the sliders button) to check Sound, Volume and Haptics, and your iPhone’s volume and Sounds & Haptics settings.
