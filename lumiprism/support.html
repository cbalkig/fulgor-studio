LumiPrism Support

Need help with LumiPrism? Write to fulgorstudio7@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can.

How do I play?

Every crystal waits for one colour; the dot in its middle shows which. Tap a mirror, splitter or prism to turn it and guide the lasers so each crystal gets exactly its colour. Light mixes like light: red and green make yellow, red and blue make magenta, green and blue make cyan, and all three make white. Tap a crystal to see what it needs, or the (?) button for every piece.

Why do I run out of taps?

Each level gives a little more energy than the fewest taps that solve it, so it rewards a moment of thought rather than tapping at random. If you run out, Try again starts the level fresh. The first levels are generous while you learn.

I’m stuck

Use an angle lock (the gold padlock): it turns one piece to its right angle and locks it there. You get a free lock every day.

Restoring a purchase

Open the store (the lightning button) and tap “Restore purchases”, or use Settings.

I reinstalled the game

If you are signed in to iCloud, your level, stars and settings come back on their own within a few seconds.

No sound or vibration

Open Settings (the sliders button) to check Sound, Volume and Haptics, and your iPhone’s volume and Sounds & Haptics settings.
