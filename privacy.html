Our Privacy Principles

Last updated: November 3, 2026

  • No account
  • On-device
  • No tracking
  • No ads
  • No servers of ours

Summary at a glance

Every Fulgor Studio app is built so that your information stays with you. We do not run accounts or servers, we do not use analytics or advertising, and we do not sell or share personal data.

What every app has in common

  • No accounts. You never sign up, so we hold no profile, email or password for you.
  • On-device data. Settings, progress and anything the app works with are stored on your device and removed when you delete the app.
  • Only the permissions needed. An app asks for access (like Photos or notifications) only for a feature you use, and you can change it any time in Settings.
  • No tracking, no ads. No analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, no tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple through the App Store. Our apps use RevenueCat to check which purchases you own; it receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history, never your name, email or payment details. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If anything changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will be described here before they take effect.
