# HaptiZen Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

HaptiZen is published by Fulgor Studio ("we"). Contact: fulgorstudio7@gmail.com

## What stays on your device
Your sound setting, the textures you have tried and your daily ritual progress are stored only on your device. Motion sensor readings are used only to notice a shake and are never stored or sent anywhere. We have no accounts and no servers of our own.

## Advertising
HaptiZen shows no ads and does not track you.

## Purchases
Purchases are handled by the App Store and managed through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history. We never see your payment details. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## Children
HaptiZen is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes
We will post any changes to this policy on this page.
