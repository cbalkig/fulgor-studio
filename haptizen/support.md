# HaptiZen Support

Need help with HaptiZen? Write to fulgorstudio7@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can.

**Restoring a purchase:** open any locked texture and tap "Restore purchase".

**No sound:** check your volume and the sound button at the top of the screen.

**Vibration:** it needs an iPhone with vibration turned on in Settings > Sounds & Haptics.
