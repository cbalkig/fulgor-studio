ChromaFold Support

Need help with ChromaFold? Write to fulgorstudio7@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can.

How do I play?

Tap a glass plate, or drag it towards the grid, to fold it over its hinge. Overlapping glass mixes: cyan and yellow make green, cyan and magenta make purple, magenta and yellow make orange, all three make a bright prism, and the same colour twice makes a deeper shade. Match the small target pattern at the top. Tap a folded plate’s dotted outline to open it again.

I’m stuck

Use a hint (the light bulb): it opens any wrong plate and folds the next right one. You get a free hint every day.

Restoring a purchase

Open the store (the diamond button) and tap “Restore purchases”, or use Settings.

No sound or vibration

Open Settings (the sliders button) to check Sound, Volume and Haptics, and your iPhone’s volume and Sounds & Haptics settings.

Still stuck? Email fulgorstudio7@gmail.com. Read the ChromaFold privacy policy.
