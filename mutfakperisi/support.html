Mutfak Perisi Support

Need help with Mutfak Perisi? Write to fulgorstudio7@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can.

How does the fairy pick tonight’s dishes?

She looks at who is at the table (tap faces on the home screen to mark who is out), everyone’s allergies, diets and dislikes, your favourites and what you cook often, what is already in your pantry and your home town. Dishes with an allergen for someone at the table are never suggested.

How are portions worked out?

One adult woman’s portion counts as 1; children, teenagers, older people, athletes, pregnant and breastfeeding members and guests are scaled from that. Tap the portions bubble on the home screen to see the sum. You can also change portions on any recipe.

How do I make a shopping list?

Add dishes to your menu, open the Menu tab, tick what you already have (or tap “Biraz var” to say how much is left) and open the list. It is grouped by market aisle and rounded to real pack sizes; send it with WhatsApp in one tap.

Can I use cups and spoons from another country?

Yes. In Settings → Ölçüler choose Turkey, US & Canada, UK, Australia or Europe. Cups, spoons, oven temperatures and pack sizes change, and the app warns you about differences such as European baking powder packets.

The microphone doesn’t work

Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition for Mutfak Perisi in the iPhone Settings app. You can always type the same sentence instead.

Can I change my palate preference later?

Yes. Family tab → Settings (gear) → Damak tadı. You can also change your name, home town and units there.

Restoring Pro

Open the Pro screen and tap “Satın alımı geri yükle” while signed in to the same Apple ID.
