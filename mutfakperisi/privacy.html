Privacy Policy

Mutfak Perisi Privacy

Last updated: November 4, 2026 · Published by Fulgor Studio

  • No account
  • Stays on your device
  • No tracking
  • No ads
  • No notifications

Summary at a glance

Mutfak Perisi helps you plan and cook meals. Your family profiles, pantry, favourites and guest book stay on your iPhone. We have no accounts and no servers of our own, and nothing you enter is sent to us.

What the app accesses

  • Microphone and speech recognition (optional): when you tap the microphone, the app listens for one sentence, such as “my husband is out tonight” or “next step”. Apple’s speech recognition turns it into text; depending on your device, Apple may process the audio on its servers under Apple’s privacy policy. The text is used only on your device to update tonight’s table, your pantry or the cooking step, and is not stored or sent to us. You can type instead and never grant this permission.
  • Spoken steps: recipe steps are read aloud with your iPhone’s built-in voice, on the device.
  • Sharing: when you share a shopping list, it goes only to the app or person you pick in the system share sheet, such as WhatsApp.
  • Mutfak Perisi does not access your photos, contacts, location, camera or health data. You choose your home town from a list; the app never asks for your location.

Health and diet labels

Allergy, diet and health labels (for example gluten-free, low salt or suitable for babies) are for information only and are not medical advice. Always check package labels and follow your doctor’s or dietitian’s advice.

What stays on your device

Your name, home town, measuring units, palate preference, family members (names, ages, diets, allergies and dislikes), pantry, menu, favourites, cooking history and guest book are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Purchases

Mutfak Kraliçesi Pro is a one-time purchase through Apple’s App Store. The app talks to Apple directly to show the price, take payment and restore purchases; we never see your name, email or payment details, and no third party is involved.

Mutfak Perisi does not connect to any server of ours. Apart from Apple’s services, it works entirely offline.

Notifications

Mutfak Perisi does not send notifications. Kitchen timers run while the app is open.

Children

Mutfak Perisi is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If anything changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will be described here before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email fulgorstudio7@gmail.com. We read every message.
