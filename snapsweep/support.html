I emptied the trash but my storage didn’t change.

iOS keeps deleted photos in Photos › Albums › Recently Deleted for 30 days. Open it and tap Delete All to get the space back right away. On Mac, files you swept sit in the Trash until you empty it.

Restoring a purchase

Open the paywall from Settings in SnapSweep and tap “Restore purchase”.

SnapSweep can’t see my photos

Go to Settings › SnapSweep › Photos and choose Full Access.

Vibration

It needs an iPhone with vibration turned on in Settings › Sounds & Haptics.
