# SnapSweep Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

SnapSweep is published by Fulgor Studio ("we"). Contact: fulgorstudio7@gmail.com

## Your photos stay on your device
SnapSweep reads your photo library only on your iPhone to show you photos and videos and their sizes. Your photos are never uploaded, copied or sent anywhere. We have no accounts and no servers of our own.

## Sharing
When you tap Share, the item goes only to the app or person you pick in the iOS share sheet. SnapSweep itself sends nothing.

## Deleting
When you empty the trash, SnapSweep asks iOS to delete the items you chose. iOS asks you to confirm and keeps them in Recently Deleted for 30 days. If you use iCloud Photos, iOS removes them from iCloud too.

## What stays on your device
Your trash list, the photos you chose to keep, your daily swipe count, the space you have freed and your settings are stored only on your device.

## Purchases
Purchases are handled by the App Store and managed through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history. We never see your payment details. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## Notifications
The daily reminder is scheduled on your device, only if you turn it on.

## Children
SnapSweep is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes
We will post any changes to this policy on this page.
