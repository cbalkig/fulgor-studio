# SnapSweep Support

Need help with SnapSweep? Write to fulgorstudio7@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can.

**I emptied the trash but my storage didn't change.** iOS keeps deleted photos in Photos › Albums › Recently Deleted for 30 days. Open it and tap Delete All to get the space back right away.

**Restoring a purchase:** open the paywall from Settings in SnapSweep and tap "Restore purchase".

**SnapSweep can't see my photos:** go to Settings › SnapSweep › Photos and choose Full Access.

**Vibration:** it needs an iPhone with vibration turned on in Settings › Sounds & Haptics.
