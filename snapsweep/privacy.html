Privacy Policy

SnapSweep Privacy

Last updated: November 3, 2026 · Published by Fulgor Studio

  • No account
  • Stays on your device
  • No tracking
  • No ads
  • No servers of ours

Summary at a glance

SnapSweep helps you clear out photos on iPhone and Mac, and clutter files on Mac. Everything happens on your device. Your photos and files are never uploaded, copied or sent anywhere, and we have no accounts and no servers of our own.

What the app accesses

Photos (iPhone and Mac)

With your permission, SnapSweep reads your photo library on your device to show you photos and videos and their sizes. If an item is stored in iCloud Photos, Apple’s system may fetch it from iCloud so you can view it; that is handled by Apple, not by us.

Files (Mac only)

  • SnapSweep only looks in places you grant: your Downloads folder, plus any folder you choose to add (such as your Home folder). If you ask it to remove unused apps, macOS asks you to allow access to Applications first.
  • It reads file information such as names, sizes and dates (created, added and last opened) to find caches, build leftovers, forgotten downloads, large old files and apps you never use.
  • It never uploads, copies or sends your files anywhere.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the item goes only to the app or person you pick in the system share sheet. SnapSweep itself sends nothing.

Deleting

  • Photos: when you empty SnapSweep’s trash, it asks the system to delete the items you chose. You are asked to confirm, and the items stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days. If you use iCloud Photos, they are removed from iCloud too.
  • Files on Mac: files and apps you choose are moved to the macOS Trash, where you can put them back. If you choose to empty them from SnapSweep, it asks you to confirm and then permanently deletes only the items SnapSweep moved; the rest of your Trash is left alone.

What stays on your device

Your trash list, the photos you chose to keep, your daily swipe count, the space you have freed, your settings and, on Mac, the list of folders you allowed are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. SnapSweep uses RevenueCat to check which purchases you own; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history. It does not receive your name, email or payment details, and neither do we. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

This is the only reason SnapSweep connects to the internet.

Notifications

SnapSweep can send a daily reminder, only if you turn it on. It is scheduled on your device and you can turn it off at any time.

Children

SnapSweep is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If anything changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will be described here before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email fulgorstudio7@gmail.com. We read every message.

SnapSweep follows the Fulgor Studio privacy principles shared by all our apps. Need help? Visit SnapSweep Support.
